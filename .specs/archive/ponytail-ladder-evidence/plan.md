## Objective

Implement Ponytail's complete seven-rung solution ladder in the owned Pi and Claude instruction surfaces, make the Pi overengineering screens operate on production-shaped session records, and publish an isolated three-arm comparison against the pre-plan and pre-ladder baselines without weakening existing safety boundaries or removing upstream tracking.

## Completion Evidence

- Evidence: Pi and Claude apply the ordered seven-rung ladder with its comprehension and safety rules; production-shaped tool-result fixtures and one bounded live aggregate expose non-null structural tool fields without returning transcript content; and a preregistered, isolated benchmark records correctness, safety, selected mechanism, diff size, churn, tokens, and cost for `39537c6a`, `09d7bfa27b9e4bb4c8da1017dfe3c99ea5b2837a`, and the completed implementation.
- Fails when: Any ladder rung or safety carve-out is absent or not activated for implementation selection, real Pi tool results still have null structural projections, analytics output exposes arguments/results or source paths, benchmark arms share mutable state or differ in model/task/tool conditions, correctness or safety failures are hidden by aggregate size/cost results, or the Ponytail tracking mechanism is removed.

## Boundaries

- In scope: The canonical Pi and Claude `analysis-workflow` solution-selection instructions; the Ponytail capability dispositions; typed convenience projection of nested Pi session tool-result metadata; production-shaped analytics regression tests and the RG screening query; and one bounded three-arm benchmark with durable sanitized findings.
- Out of scope: Ponytail runtime intensity modes, persistent defaults, marker files, comment-based debt state, new dependencies, scheduler state, changes to correctness review or plan lifecycle, removal or redesign of upstream tracking, a general benchmark platform, and unrelated Pi, Claude, Herdr, subagent, or dotfiles work.
- Preserve: Existing instruction ownership, client boundaries, `session_entries` source ID and one-record-per-JSONL-row semantics, complete `record` access controls, row/byte/time and external-filesystem query bounds, transcript-versus-metadata privacy boundaries, safety and accessibility carve-outs, unrelated working-tree changes, and default `/do-it` worktree, merge, and cleanup behavior.
- Assumptions: Benchmark runs use one available canonical developer model and effort pinned identically across all cells; the resolved provider/model, effort, Pi version, instruction snapshot, enabled extensions/tools, fixture hash, and repository commit are recorded, and execution stops rather than substituting a different model or contaminated configuration.
