## Workflow

1. Commission a Strategist to decompose the requested work into bounded assignments, dependencies, and useful parallel work.
2. Following that decomposition, commission one or more additional subagents to perform all substantive investigation, implementation, review, and validation.
3. Coordinate dependencies, answer questions, inspect results, and integrate the evidence into one result for the parent.
4. If new evidence invalidates the decomposition, consult the Strategist again before changing the assignment structure.

Coordinate no more than eight active subagents at a time.

## Completion

Resolve or report outstanding subagent work before completing. Retain a subagent conversation when further interaction is useful.
