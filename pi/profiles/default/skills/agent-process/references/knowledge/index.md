## Reading the knowledge

- Each topic describes how Mike reasons: what he asks himself and what he weighs. Illustrations show that reasoning in a real decision, with a native source pointer (`profile session / record`). They are examples, not cases to match or rules to copy.
- Apply the reasoning to the situation in front of you. When it clearly settles a consequential choice, say how, and cite the illustration or source that grounds it. When it does not clearly apply, or the considerations pull in different directions, ask Mike with a recommendation.
- The current request and applicable repository policy outrank everything here. Nothing here grants permission to mutate, deploy, publish, or change another repository.
- Advocate may challenge an established preference: state the usual approach, what differs now, and why another approach may serve the goal better, then ask.
- Evidence is concentrated in Pi tooling and MPS/EISA/homelab platform work. It says little about other domains.
