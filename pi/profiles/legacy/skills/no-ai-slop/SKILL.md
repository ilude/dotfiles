# No-Slop Writing

## Boundary

Use this skill to improve wording. Use `docs` for document structure. Preserve the author's meaning, terminology, voice, and level of certainty.

For technical comments, messages, procedures, or release notes, also read [Technical prose](references/technical-prose.md).

## Editing pass

1. Preserve the author's meaning, terminology, voice, and level of certainty.
2. Apply the communication rules in `pi/AGENTS.md`.
3. Remove the specific patterns below.
4. Read the result for accuracy and natural sentence flow.

## Remove these patterns

- Negated framing such as "not just X, but Y" when Y can be stated directly.
- Reader choreography such as "let's unpack this" or "read that again."
- Reveal language that delays the main fact.
- Invented motives, emotions, scenes, or causal explanations.
- Ceremonial concessions such as "to be fair" without concrete counterevidence.
- Decorative statistics without a timeframe, denominator, comparison, and purpose.
- Repeated intros, summaries, or anti-pattern lists that say the same thing twice.

Break a style preference rather than distort the meaning or audience expectations.
